What we do

StreamWorld follows streamers playing GTA 6 and turns their best moments into vertical short videos. Every clip gets our own hook, context, comment and a question for the community. Clips credit and link the original streamer.

StreamWorld Uploader

To publish our own clips, StreamWorld uses a small private tool ("StreamWorld Uploader") that uploads videos to StreamWorld's own accounts on YouTube, Instagram, Facebook and X. It is used only by the StreamWorld team and does not offer sign-in to anyone else.
