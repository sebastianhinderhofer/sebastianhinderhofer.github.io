Scope

This policy covers the website of StreamWorld and "StreamWorld Uploader", a private tool used only by the operator of StreamWorld to publish short video clips to StreamWorld's own accounts on YouTube, Instagram, Facebook and X.

YouTube API Services

StreamWorld Uploader uses YouTube API Services to upload videos to the StreamWorld YouTube channel. By using it, you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Data accessed and stored

  • OAuth access and refresh tokens for StreamWorld's own YouTube channel (scopes: upload videos, read own channel name), and access tokens for StreamWorld's own Facebook Page, Instagram account and X account.
  • The video files, titles, descriptions and hashtags being published, and the IDs of published videos (to avoid duplicate uploads).

The tool does not access, collect or store data of other users. It does not read comments, messages, subscriptions or profiles of anyone else.

How data is used and shared

Data is used only to publish StreamWorld's own videos. Tokens and logs are stored locally on the operator's computer and are never sold, used for advertising, or shared with third parties. Videos and their metadata are sent only to the platform they are published on (YouTube, Instagram, Facebook, X).

Cookies

This website does not set cookies and does not use tracking.

Retention and deletion

Upload logs are kept for as long as needed to avoid duplicate posts. Google access can be revoked at any time on the Google security settings page; Meta access under Facebook → Settings → Business Integrations; X access under X → Settings → Connected apps. To request deletion of locally stored data, email r.horden@web.de – deletion is completed within 30 days.

Changes

Changes to this policy are published on this page with a new date.

Deutsch

„StreamWorld Uploader“ ist ein privates Werkzeug, mit dem der Betreiber von StreamWorld ausschließlich eigene Kurzvideos auf den eigenen Konten bei YouTube, Instagram, Facebook und X veröffentlicht. Es nutzt die YouTube-API-Dienste; es gelten die Nutzungsbedingungen von YouTube und die Datenschutzerklärung von Google.

Gespeichert werden nur Zugriffsschlüssel für die eigenen Konten, die zu veröffentlichenden Videos mit Titel, Beschreibung und Hashtags sowie die IDs veröffentlichter Beiträge – lokal auf dem Rechner des Betreibers. Daten anderer Personen werden nicht abgerufen. Nichts wird verkauft, für Werbung genutzt oder an Dritte weitergegeben. Diese Website setzt keine Cookies.

Den Zugriff kannst du jederzeit widerrufen: Google-Sicherheitseinstellungen, Facebook → Business-Integrationen, X → Verbundene Apps. Löschung lokal gespeicherter Daten auf Anfrage an r.horden@web.de innerhalb von 30 Tagen.
